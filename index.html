Privacy Policy

Koi Fish — Job Filter & Sorter for LinkedIn — Chrome Extension

This extension is designed to be as privacy-preserving as possible. It runs entirely within your browser and does not operate any external server.

What the extension does

  • It reads the LinkedIn job feed content that is already visible to you on the page in order to display applicant counts and to enable sorting and filtering of job postings.
  • It stores your block/keep preferences locally in your browser using the chrome.storage API, so your choices persist between visits.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not send your data to any third party, and does not sell or share data with anyone.
  • It does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

Data storage and network activity

Your block/keep preferences and filter settings are stored locally on your device via chrome.storage and are never sent anywhere.

To display applicant counts and job details, the extension makes background requests directly to LinkedIn's own servers using your existing logged-in session — the same data LinkedIn's own website already loads for you. No data is ever sent to any server other than LinkedIn's.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the extension's GitHub repository.
